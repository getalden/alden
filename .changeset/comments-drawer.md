Your comments collect in a drawer beside the briefing, so it's always clear where they'll post. **Comments · 3** (or `s`) in a PR's toolbar opens it: your drafts grouped by where they go, **On lines** (file and line or range) and **In the review body** (general notes), then **Add a general note**, the review body, and the verdict with **Send review** always in view at the bottom, with the same summary before anything posts. **Comment** on a place, or `c`, opens one editor there that names its target ("Comment on py/orders/pricing.py, line 4"). **Suggest a change** adds code that posts as a suggested change the author can accept on GitHub, and **Comment on a line…** takes a range like `4–9`. The drawer stays as you left it for the window, and becomes a full-width sheet in narrow windows. It replaces the Review panel at the bottom of the briefing, in the web UI and the desktop app.
