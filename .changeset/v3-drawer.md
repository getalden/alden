The Comments drawer has the v3 design: Drafts and Conversation tabs, cards that start with who wrote each draft (you, Alden or Claude Code) and end with its state, Alden's suggestions dashed until you keep them, and "Alden writes" to pick the style Alden drafts in for a repo (Terse, Concise, Detailed or Teaching). Drag its edge to resize it (320 to 760 pixels, remembered), double-click to reset, and `s` folds it to a rail. An outdated draft says which head its lines changed at and offers "Move to new lines" to where its code went now (or "Pick a line"), and the local API lists that as `movedTo` and `changedIn`. "Review and send" opens a summary that counts what posts and what stays behind, leaves outdated drafts out instead of refusing, and on Approve offers "Look first" for each place Alden flagged that you didn't comment on; a sent review says so at the bottom of the window.
