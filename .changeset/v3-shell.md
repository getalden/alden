The web UI and the desktop app take on the v3 design's frame. The top bar is 44px, with Queue, My changes, Memory and Stats, today's spend ("$1.84 of $5.00 today"), and your avatar, whose menu has Settings, Export feedback and Sign out (which removes Alden's stored GitHub sign-in, as `alden auth logout` does). The queue column is 340px: one search box ("Search 142 PRs"), one Filter button with every filter and the sort in a popover, and groups for Your turn, Ranked for you and Fast-track, with two-line rows (title and wait, then CI, repo, author and plain flags such as re-review, ✎ 2, XL, draft, agent or bot); why a PR ranks where it does, and Useful, Not useful and Dismiss, show on the selected row. A PR's header reads the briefing in three numbered steps, 1 Brief, 2 Walk through (`g`) and 3 Diff (`d`), beside Comments and Draft my review, and its age line says "briefed 30m ago · main +4 commits" with Brief again and Export. The model line there ("sonnet-5 · medium ▾") opens Tune: the model by provider, how Alden writes, effort, callers, the per-brief cap, and a question for the model about the PR, answered as it streams. In the desktop app the top bar is the window's title bar. Light mode is derived from the new dark palette.
