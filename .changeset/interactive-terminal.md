`alden` on its own in a terminal opens an interactive view: your ranked queue (search with `/`, open with `o`, refresh with `r`), and for any PR its briefing, where `1`–`9` go to a place, `u`/`n` mark it useful or not, `c` writes a draft comment on it, `m` runs the model after saying what it may cost, and `s` shows exactly what `alden send` would post, sent only after Enter and `y`. In a pipe, in CI or with `TERM=dumb`, `alden` prints its help as before, and every command is unchanged. See [Commands](https://getalden.dev/docs/commands#alden-interactive).
