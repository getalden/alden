Alden drafts your review when a model is set up. The briefing's own model call (any model: your own key, a cloud provider or a local model, on every plan) also drafts a comment, and a suggested change when the fix is small and certain, for up to five of its medium and high findings on changed lines. They wait in the Comments drawer marked "Drafted by Alden", with Keep, Edit and Drop, and Keep all / Drop all; the briefing marks each finding "Draft ready". Nothing is sent until you keep or edit a draft and send your review ("2 drafts by Alden not kept won't be sent"), and a draft never approves. Briefing the PR again replaces only the drafts you didn't touch, and a PR with text aimed at AI reviewers gets none, with the reason in the briefing. Keeping or editing one counts once as a comment near the finding's place, and dropping one counts a little the other way (never for secrets or AI-aimed text). `alden review` says "Alden drafted 3 comments — see them with `alden comment <pr>`", `alden comment <pr> --keep <id|all>` keeps them, and **Draft comments for me** in Settings (`drafts.auto`, user config only) turns it off, which leaves the model's prompt exactly as before.
