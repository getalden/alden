The web UI and desktop app read a PR in one direction, as in the v3 design. The brief opens with what the PR does, then the places that need your eyes as cards (E1, E2… with how severe, where and why); the first is open with its code, **Comment**, **Walk through from here**, and Useful, Not useful and Dismiss. The model's other findings, the checks, what wasn't checked and the files changed fold into four rows with a line each, and how your feedback shaped the brief is one line under them. **Walk through** (`g`) takes the places one at a time in place of the brief, with step dots, the code, **Look at these too**, **Comment here**, Previous and Next, and **Finish: review your comments** on the last step. The Diff tab says which file you're reading ("file 2 of 6"), sums up each file's places and drafts in its header, and shows places, your drafts and review threads as cards under their lines.
