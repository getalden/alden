Alden learns from the reviews you send, not only from Useful and Not useful. A comment near a flagged place counts toward that check's lesson (2 when the review requests changes or the comment suggests a change, 1 for a plain comment), approving past a place Alden knows you saw counts 0.2 the other way (never for secrets, AI-aimed text or high and critical flags, and never enough to quieten a check without a Not useful of yours), a comment where Alden flagged nothing is noted as a miss, and the code areas you comment in rank higher in your queue. One review can't teach a lesson on its own: a PR counts once per topic, at its strongest signal. `alden send` says what the review moved ("2 of 3 (2 from your review of acme/shop#412): one more useful on untested-change flags…"), `alden memory` and `alden_memory` name the reviews a lesson came from, and `alden feedback` counts review signals apart from your clicks. Turn it off with `learning.fromReviews = false`. This replaces 0.4's "useful via comment", which still counts.
