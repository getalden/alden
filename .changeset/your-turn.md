**Your turn** in the queue: the PRs you reviewed where something happened you haven't seen (replies to your comments, threads resolved, your comments made outdated by a push), at the top of the web UI's and the desktop app's queue and of `alden queue`, each with one line ("Ana replied to 2 of your comments · 1 resolved") and when it happened. Open one and it goes to the PR, with the Comments drawer's Conversation open on the thread the news is in; once opened, it leaves Your turn. A PR that's still in your queue (a re-review is always there, under Needs you with its Re-review label) gets the news on its own row instead, so no PR is listed twice. It shares its news with the desktop app's notifications: a notification you've seen still leaves the PR under Your turn until you open it, and opening it first means you aren't notified. A PR's follow-up level applies: Off hides its news, Re-review only leaves just the re-review row. The web UI and `alden queue` now run the same follow-up check as the desktop app with its limits (one read-only request at most once a minute, and a PR's threads read only when it changed), and nothing when every level is Off. `alden queue --json` has the group as `yourTurn`, and each row's `followUp.news`; `alden open` takes a PR's name too (`alden open acme/shop#12`).
