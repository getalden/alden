Alden Cloud (beta)

The model for Alden's briefings, without an API key. Alden Cloud isn't built yet: this page says what we plan, so you can tell us whether you want it.

What it will be

Sign in at getalden.dev with GitHub and the model just works: no Anthropic key, no setup. Everything else stays as it is today, on your machine and free: the queue, the checks, the callers and what Alden learns about you. Your own key keeps working on every plan.

How it will handle your code

  • Only model calls go through Alden's service. Alden still reads your local clones, and what it learns about you stays in ~/.alden.
  • The diff passes through to Anthropic and isn't stored.
  • Likely secrets are removed before anything leaves your machine, as they are today with your own key (see Privacy and data).
  • Hosted in the EU and the US.
