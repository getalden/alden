Runs on your machine, on your terms. Nothing reaches GitHub until you send it.

On your machineThe queue, briefings and what Alden learns about you stay in ~/.alden.
Your GitHub sign-inAlden reads PRs as you and posts only the review you send. It never approves or pushes.
Your model, or noneAn Anthropic or OpenAI key, Bedrock, Vertex, Foundry, or a local model through Ollama. Checks run without one. No key? Alden Cloud is planned.
Spend caps$0.50 per briefing and $10 per day by default, checked before every call.
Drafts never sent untouchedEvery drafted comment waits for you to keep, edit or drop it.
Open sourceApache-2.0 when the beta ends. The single-developer app stays free.
